Hello, I am Frank Adler G. Ong. Welcome to a record of an ordinary person learning to navigate life, service, and the demanding first steps of medical school.

I do not claim an extraordinary story. What matters to me is continuing to learn and using what I know to help the people around me.

03 / REFLECTION

Learning through
challenge and connection.

I may not remember every date from Biochemistry, but I remember how the subject made me feel: challenged most of the time, yet genuinely glad to be there. I entered the class hesitant. Over time, “We will make Biochemistry fun for you” stopped sounding like a promise and started feeling true.

The enjoyment did not come from the subject becoming easy. It came from discovering ideas I had never encountered, working through concepts that tested my patience, and learning beside people who gradually became friends. We taught one another in ways I had not expected, turning confusion into conversations and difficult lessons into shared progress.

Biochemistry has been bittersweet. Every quiz or examination carries the possibility of failure, but each one also shows me what I still need to understand. My journey is only beginning. I hope to remain present, keep learning, and appreciate every part of the time I have in this class.

“The subject did not become easy. I learned to find meaning in the difficulty.”
